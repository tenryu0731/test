# LAST LIGHT — 60-second wordless 2D animated short

Absolutely no words, text, speech, or vocal syllables anywhere (visuals or audio).
Duration: exactly 60.000 s. Video 1920x1080 @ 30 fps. Audio 48 kHz stereo.

## Logline
The sky is losing its stars. The very last one falls to a dead, grey desert.
A tiny round creature ("Pip") finds it, shields it through a sandstorm, and when
it is almost extinguished, breathes life back into it. The star becomes a seed,
grows into a luminous tree, and its blossoms rise to re-light the entire sky.

## Characters / motifs
- **The Star**: warm gold (#FFD27A core, #FF9E3D halo). Pulses like a heartbeat.
- **Pip**: small round cream-white creature, big dark eyes, a tiny leaf sprout on
  its head. Communicates only with bounces, blinks, squeaks (non-verbal chirps).
- **World**: starts cold & desaturated (deep indigo / grey-blue), ends warm, lush,
  multicoloured (teal grass, magenta/pink blossoms, gold light).
