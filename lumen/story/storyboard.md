# LUMEN — storyboard and master timeline

60 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, wordless. Every cue below is the shared clock for picture, music and SFX.
Key: D minor → D major (Picardy turn at the cross), plagal "Amen" ending.

## S1 Creation (0.0 – 9.0)
| t | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 – 1.5 | Black. | Sub drone fades in from silence (D1/A1). |
| 1.5 | A single point of light appears at centre. | One high glass/bell ping (D6). |
| 1.5 – 3.2 | The point breathes and grows, faint radial rays. | Rising swell (reverse cymbal + choir crescendo), sharp cut at 3.2. |
| **3.2** | **"Let there be light"**: white flash, shockwave ring, nebula blooms, stars scatter outward. | Tutti hit: choir "Ah" on D (open fifths, no third), timpani, deep boom, long shimmering tail. |
| 3.2 – 7.0 | Camera drifts forward through the new cosmos; light gathers into a sun and a planet. | Choir tail + string pad, shimmer. |
| 7.0 – 9.0 | Camera dives down into the planet's dark storm clouds. | Descending whoosh, wind rises, first rain fades in from 8.0. |

## S3 Prayer and the dove (19.0 – 29.0)

## S4 The way to the cross (29.0 – 39.0)
| t | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 29.0 – 30.0 | He rises. | Cloth rustle. |
| 30.0 – 36.0 | Walks toward and up the hill; glowing footprints. Rain lighter, then storm swirls up again. | Footsteps every 0.6 s; processional drum; long crescendo 33 → 38 (organ + choir + strings + timpani roll). |
